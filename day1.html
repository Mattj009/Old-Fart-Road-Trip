HAPPY 69th ANNIVERSARY JACK AND LINDA MATTHEWS!

Old Fart Travel's Rock Tour Road Trip 2.0 opens with one spectacular opening act—a mad dash across northern Arizona to one of the most breathtaking landscapes in the American Southwest.

The goal isn't just to get there.

It's to arrive in time for one of the most unforgettable sunsets of the entire vacation.

📋 TODAY'S PLAN

✈️ 8:30 a.m.
Pick up John and Robin at the Drury Inn & Suites – Phoenix Airport.
🚗 Road Trip Begins!
Destination: Monument Valley, Utah
Approximate driving time: 5 hours (plus stops).
🛒 Midday Stop – Flagstaff
Our last full-service city before entering Navajo Nation. Time to stock up on snacks, drinks, breakfast items, and anything else we'll want over the next couple of days.
🍽️ Suggested Lunch – Galaxy Diner
A classic Route 66 diner serving burgers, comfort food, and more than 100 flavors of milkshakes.
🏨 4:00 p.m. – Target Arrival
Check into The View Hotel and settle in before the evening's main event.
🌅 Sunset at Monument Valley
Golden Hour begins at 7:03 p.m.
Sunset is at 8:03 p.m.

As the sun drops lower in the sky, the Mittens and Merrick Butte begin glowing with deep reds and brilliant oranges while long shadows stretch across the valley floor. It's one of the signature moments of the entire trip.

🥪 Dinner
The View Restaurant receives mixed reviews, so we may opt for a more dependable choice by picking up sandwiches in Kayenta before arriving.
✨ Late Evening
Weather permitting, spend some time under one of the darkest night skies in America before turning in for an early night.

💡 OFT TIP

Flagstaff is the last major shopping stop before Monument Valley. Grocery prices and gas are generally better here than farther north, so it's the perfect place to stock up before heading into Navajo Nation.
