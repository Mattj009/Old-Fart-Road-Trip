🎰 LET'S MAKE THIS ONE COUNT

Our last full day of vacation will enshrine a few more adventures in the Old Fart Travel vault as future cherished memories.

Today we'll leave the gun, take the cannolis, conquer a mountain of pancakes, stroll the wonderfully weird Fremont Street Experience, and learn a little about the real history that inspired so many of our favorite mob movies—all before one last dip in the pool.

Let's make this one count.

📋 TODAY'S PLAN

There's only one proper way to begin our final full day.

Hash House a Go Go.

You've probably heard about the hubcap-sized pancakes.

Especially for first-timers.

🌺 DINNER — ZIPPY'S

We're not making it to Hawaii this summer...

...but Hawaii is coming to us.

Zippy's is more than a restaurant—it's a Hawaiian institution.

Part diner, part bakery, part local gathering place, it serves Hawaiian, Japanese, Korean, and classic American comfort food under one roof.

Las Vegas is often called the “Ninth Island,” making Zippy's feel perfectly at home here.

We can't wait to share one of our favorite Hawaiian traditions with Joan and Bill.
