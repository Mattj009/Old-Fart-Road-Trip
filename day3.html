🏜️ Canyon de Chelly National Monument
Unlike most of the places we'll visit on this trip, Canyon de Chelly is not simply a place where people once lived—it's a place where people still do.

Diné (Navajo) families continue to raise livestock, farm, and make their homes here, just as their ancestors have for nearly 5,000 years. After gathering maps at the Visitor Center, we'll drive the North and South Rim Drives, stopping at overlooks with spectacular views into the canyon.

🌈 12:30–1:00 p.m. – Petrified Forest National Park
Famous for its 225-million-year-old petrified wood and the brilliantly colored badlands of the Painted Desert, the park's scenic 28-mile road lets us experience one of Arizona's most unusual landscapes in just a couple of hours.

Bring your camera. The colors almost don't look real.

🎸 Winslow, Arizona
Have your picture taken at the famous “Standin' on the Corner” monument, enjoy an early dinner in town, and soak in one of America's great Route 66 communities.
