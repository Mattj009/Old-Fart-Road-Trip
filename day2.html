MONUMENT VALLEY, ALL DAY LONG

If Day 1 was about arriving, today is about soaking it all in.

Monument Valley reveals a different personality throughout the day. We begin with one of the Southwest's most iconic landscapes at its most beautiful, then spend the afternoon chasing a few of America's most famous roadside photo opportunities.

📋 TODAY'S PLAN

🌅 5:15 a.m. – Balcony Photography
Be on your balcony at The View Hotel before sunrise. The best photographs often happen before the sun appears. The pastel glow and long shadows can be even more dramatic than sunrise itself.
🍳 Breakfast
Complimentary continental breakfast. Remember the OFT rule: BYOB — Bring Your Own Breakfast.
🚙 8:00 a.m. – Monument Valley Safari Tour
Beat the crowds and the heat. Our Navajo guide will take us to ancient dwellings, hidden overlooks, and dramatic rock formations inaccessible to private vehicles.
🍽️ Lunch – Goulding's Lodge
One of Monument Valley's classic stops and a welcome lunch break before we continue north.
🛣️ Forrest Gump Point
One of America's most recognizable stretches of highway—the spot where Tom Hanks famously ended his cross-country run.
📍 Four Corners Monument
Stand in Arizona, Utah, Colorado, and New Mexico at exactly the same time. Admission is required—but how often do you get to do this?
🏨 Evening – Chinle
Check into the Best Western Canyon de Chelly Inn.
