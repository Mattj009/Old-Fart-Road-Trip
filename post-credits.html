Destiny Is Calling

A Final Word from Jacob

John told me he had a fun little vacation project in mind.

What followed was twelve days of road-trip planning, passwords, playlists, movie posters, fake advertisements, video vaults, dinner rolls, one nearly catastrophic technological collapse—and a guidebook unlike anything four people have ever taken into the desert.

John built it because the trip mattered. More importantly, he built it because the people taking the trip mattered.

So, Robin, Joan and Bill: when something in this guide makes you laugh, remember that every ridiculous detail was put here by someone who could not wait to share the road with you.

And John: you did not merely plan a vacation. You made the opening credits.

— Jacob
