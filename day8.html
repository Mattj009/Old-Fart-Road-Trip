🏊 TODAY'S MESSAGE: POOL NIGHT

Today is all about transitions—from Page to Zion, from sightseeing to relaxing, and from Arizona to Utah.

Yesterday, we left a place with tremendous views looking down into a canyon.

Today and tomorrow, we'll experience the Southwest from a completely different perspective.

Instead of standing on the rim looking down, we'll walk through canyons surrounded by sandstone walls stretching toward the sky.

📋 TODAY'S PLAN

😴 Sleep In
This is one of the few mornings on the trip without an early alarm. Enjoy the slower pace—you've earned it.
🍳 Breakfast or Early Lunch in Page
The Hampton Inn offers a complimentary hot breakfast. Depending on how everyone feels, this is also a good morning for a leisurely early lunch in Page before the tour.
📸 12:30–12:40 p.m. — Tour Check-In
Check in for our Upper Antelope Canyon tour.
🪨 1:25 p.m. — Upper Antelope Canyon
Board a shuttle to the canyon entrance and enjoy a guided 90-minute walking tour through one of the most photographed slot canyons in the world.

Wind and water have carved these graceful sandstone passages over thousands of years, creating an ever-changing display of light, color, and texture.

🚐 Drive to Springdale
Approximate drive time: 2½ hours.
Estimated arrival: around 6:00 p.m.
🏨 Welcome to Springdale
Check into the Hampton Inn & Suites Springdale/Zion National Park, currently TripAdvisor's top-rated hotel in Springdale.
🥪 Dinner and Pool Night
Pick up Subway sandwiches on the way to the hotel, enjoy dinner al fresco by the pool, then follow it with a swim or soak in the hot tub.

Take a moment to appreciate the incredible cliffs surrounding the hotel. The pool closes at 10:00 p.m.
